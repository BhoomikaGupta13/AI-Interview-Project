In the movie recommender system project, I first went to get all the data from IMDb website for approximately 15 movies and then I stole them in our host base in scabal database. From that database I made a content based movie recommender system using TFIDF idea of vectorization and cosine similarity to find similar movies based on John Ray director. And top 10 relevant movies are displayed to the user, according to their interest from lots of determinants.
